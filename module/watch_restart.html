Supervise watcher children and restart them on nonzero exit.

Leading CLI flags override manifest supervisor defaults. A zero child exit or user interruption ends supervision; other exits restart after the delay, subject to the restart limit. Command failures only reach the supervisor if the watcher itself exits nonzero, as in one-shot mode. Child generations share the parent's selected log format and use distinct cycle-ID prefixes.
