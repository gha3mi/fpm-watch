Assemble watcher options, typed fpm settings, and a serialized command.

Built-in defaults are followed by [extra.fpm-watch] and leading watcher flags. All tokens after build, test, or run are retained for execution; recognized fpm options also populate the settings used for graph construction. Model-option scanning stops at --. Parsing uses argv tokens, not shell text.
